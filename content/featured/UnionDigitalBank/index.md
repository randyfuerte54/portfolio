A mobile-first digital bank in your pocket: open an account, save, pay bills, and send money between UnionDigital accounts, to UnionBank, and to other banks, secured with in-app OTP and blockchain-based identity verification. It was the first mobile digital banking app in the Philippines, shipped in 7 months. I was a pioneer developer on the transfer features and POC lead for OTP and identity verification, writing native Kotlin and Swift where needed.
