A construction safety application used by Australian construction teams to improve on-site safety, compliance, and emergency response. Delivered evacuation workflows, Single Sign-On, a Plant Directory for equipment management, and landscape mode for tablet usability, while relaunching and stabilising unit test coverage across the legacy codebase.
